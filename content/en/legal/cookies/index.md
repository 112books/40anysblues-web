## What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites store on the user's device in order to remember preferences and make browsing easier.

## Cookies used by this website

This website **does not use tracking or advertising cookies**.

| Type | Name | Purpose | Duration |
|------|------|---------|----------|
| Functional | `cookies_ok` | Remembers that you have accepted the cookie notice (localStorage, not strictly a cookie) | Until deleted manually |
| Functional | `blues40_auth` | Stores the authentication state (localStorage, not strictly a cookie) | Until deleted manually |
| Functional | `nl_seen` | Remembers whether you have already seen the invitation to subscribe to the newsletter (localStorage) | Until deleted manually |

This data is stored in the browser's **localStorage**, not in cookies. It is not transmitted to any server, nor shared with third parties.

## External services

- **Brevo and Formspree**: only activated when you submit a form (subscription or contact).
- **Giscus / GitHub**: only activated in the visitors' book. When they load, GitHub may set its own cookies or local storage. See the [GitHub privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
- **GoatCounter**: the statistics are anonymous and **do not use cookies**.

## Analytics

GoatCounter processes only aggregated and anonymous data (country, browser, page visited). No individual user can be identified.

## How to manage cookies

You can delete the localStorage at any time from your browser's developer tools (F12 → Application → Local Storage).

To configure or block cookies in your browser:
- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Preferences → Privacy and security
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy
