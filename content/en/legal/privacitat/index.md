## Data we collect

This website collects personal data in the following cases:

### Contact form
Fields collected: name and email address. Purpose: to reply to the enquiry sent. Legal basis: the user's consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR). The data are transmitted to and processed by **Formspree, Inc.** ([Formspree privacy policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/)).

### Newsletter subscription
Fields collected: email address and, optionally, stage name. Purpose: to send communications about the project *40 Anys de blues a Barcelona*. Legal basis: explicit consent by double opt-in (art. 6.1.a GDPR). The data are managed through **Brevo SAS** ([Brevo privacy policy](https://www.brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy/)). You may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link included in every communication.

### Musicians' register
Fields collected: stage name and email address. Purpose: to verify the identity of the musicians who appear in the project and to send them exclusive communications. Legal basis: explicit consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR). The data are managed through **Brevo SAS**.

### Communications to participating musicians
On the occasion of the presentation of the book and of the exhibition “40 Anys de blues a Barcelona”, we send a single informative email to the people portrayed in it. Legal basis: **legitimate interest** in informing about the very project in which they have taken part (art. 6.1.f GDPR). Every communication includes a **one-click unsubscribe link**; once unsubscribed, recipients will receive no further emails. Anyone who wishes to keep receiving news may subscribe voluntarily to the newsletter (consent, art. 6.1.a).

### Guestbook comments
To leave a comment in the guestbook you need a **GitHub** account. The comment and the username become public. Comments are managed through **Giscus** and **GitHub**. Legal basis: consent upon publishing the comment.

### Analytics
This website uses **GoatCounter**, a statistics system that **does not use cookies** and processes only aggregated, anonymous data (page visited, country, browser). No individual user can be identified.

## Data retention

Contact data are kept for as long as necessary to deal with the enquiry. Subscription data are kept for as long as the subscription remains active. Guestbook comments are kept for as long as they are published.

## Users' rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data
- **Rectify** inaccurate data
- **Erase** your data (‘right to be forgotten’)
- **Object** to processing
- **Data portability**

To exercise any of these rights, please contact hola@112books.eu stating which right you wish to exercise and enclosing a copy of your identity document.
