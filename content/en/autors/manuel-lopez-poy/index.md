**Manuel López Poy** —Sarria (Lugo), 1958— is a journalist and the author of books such as _Camino a la libertad, historia social del blues_; _Todo Blues_; _Los días azules, historias de blues_; _Entre el cielo y el infierno, efemérides del blues_, and _Blues, la novela gráfica_.

He edits the _Anuario de Blues_, published by the Societat de Blues de Barcelona, and is the scriptwriter of documentaries such as _Barnablues, la història del blues a Barcelona_ and _Hondarribia Crossroads_. He is also the author of works of fiction such as the collection of stories _Aires de tormenta_, and is a writer of graphic novels.

For _40 anys de Blues a Barcelona_ he has written the biographies of all the musicians portrayed in it, combining journalistic rigour with blues passion to give voice to a scene he has known at first hand since its origins.
