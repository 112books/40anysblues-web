**Lluís Souto** (Terrassa, 1970) is a harmonica player and one of the essential names on the Barcelona blues scene. A profound connoisseur of the genre, he has devoted decades to studying its history and evolution, and he is the author of classes and lectures on the blues harmonica. For _40 anys de Blues a Barcelona_ he has served as proofreader, a task that follows naturally from his career as a musician and populariser of Barcelona blues.
