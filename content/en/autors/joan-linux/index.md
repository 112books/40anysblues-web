**Joan “Linux” Martínez i Serres** (Barcelona 1972) is a photographer and editor. For more than two decades he has documented the blues scene and other live musics, notable among his work being his role as official photographer of the [Festival de Blues de Barcelona](https://www.festivalbluesbarcelona.com/es). A driving force behind several publishing projects related to photography and culture, he is the creator and coordinator of **_40 anys de blues a Barcelona_**, a work conceived in 2020 that combines oral memory, historical documentation and photographic portraiture. All the photographs of the musicians included in this publication have been taken by the author in the studio and using photochemical photographic processes.
