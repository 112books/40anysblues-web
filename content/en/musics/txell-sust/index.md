As the nineties were dawning, amid the media noise of the advent of the Barcelona Olympics, a young woman with a sensual and elegant voice was trying to carve out a place for herself on the city's jazz and blues scene. She succeeded fully in 1993, when she met the pianist [August Tharrats](/en/musics/august-tharrats/), with whom she has continued working ever since. Music of African-American roots had taken possession of her heart through the cinema, and by the age of twenty-two she had already become a professional singer. An absolute point of reference for jazz in the region, she has formed an intimate and fundamental part of the blues scene, collaborating with founding names such as [Amadeu Casas](/en/musics/amadeu-casas/) or the Blues Messengers. A composer and producer with two albums to her name, her vocal register and her deep knowledge of music have allowed her to travel through a wide range of genres, from the pop of her first group, Dressed in Blue, and her collaborations with [La Unión](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Uni%C3%B3n_%28banda%29), [Amaya Montero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amaia_Montero) or Los Sencillos, to musicals and work in advertising, radio and television. The blues, in her own words, is: “A school. Clear structures in which to draw melodies and stories that reach the heart. A way of life.”
