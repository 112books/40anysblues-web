He began going up on stage in 1976 in progressive rock groups, in the years of the [Rock Laietà](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_Laiet%C3%A0), and came to perform in seven groups at the same time. He arrived at the music of African-American roots at the end of the eighties, at the hands of [Big Mama Montse](/en/musics/big-mama-montse/), who taught him the language of the blues, and of [Leo Carreras](/en/musics/leo-carreras/), with whom he created [Fas Tard](/en/bands/fas-tard/), one of the pioneering bands of the Barcelona scene. He was part of the city’s pioneering jams, where he was drawn to the communion between musicians: “I liked the improvisational element that made exchange with other musicians so easy.” Years later he has continued to work with improvised music, practising free, non-idiomatic improvisation and organising series such as the Impronits or the Encontres en l’Espai-Temps, under the umbrella of “[Gràcia Territori Sonor](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gr%C3%A0cia_Territori_Sonor)”. A life dedicated to music, in the latest stage more focused on the technical and production side, in which the blues has filled him with life: “I have met a great many wonderful artists and taken part in memorable concerts. Those moments, and the bonds you end up forming, enrich you both professionally and personally.”
