Before becoming the drummer of one of the city's most charismatic bands, [The Big Jamboree](/en/bands/the-big-jamboree/), he gained experience from the early eighties playing in rock'n'roll groups, and as he gradually discovered rhythm&blues he delved ever deeper into the roots of the genre, especially [jump blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jump_blues), one of his favourite styles. In his own particular calendar of musical saints, artists such as [Louis Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Jordan), [Roy Brown](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roy_Brown), [Wynonie Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wynonie_Harris) or [Big Joe Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Joe_Turner) occupy a prominent place, which speaks clearly of his musical preferences, always centred on the styles that foreshadowed the first rock'n'roll and [rockabilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockabilly). With his musical mastery, this solid and effective drummer has become an important figure within Barcelona's scene of music of African-American origin over the last three decades. Although his preferences lean above all towards rhythm&blues, he confesses to being seduced by the genuine purity of the blues: “An authentic, sincere and simple music, without artifice”, which has also provided him with “loads of fun”.
