When Nel·lo landed at the legendary jams of [La Cova del Drac](/en/sales/la-cova-del-drac/) he had barely come of age, but he had already toured the stages of the whole country with [Los Rebeldes](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Rebeldes), the successful rock’n’roll band of the eighties. But the few square metres of that basement where the first blues gatherings were held made him approach music in a new way. Far from the big stages and the mass audiences, he found himself in a new dimension in his playing of the saxophone, with a close audience, reacting emotionally to a music he delved into through projects of very different kinds, always with the blues as a base and the saxophone — an instrument of which he is one of the country’s greatest experts as regards technique, evolution and history — as the fundamental tool of his brilliant creativity. Over the last two decades he has become one of the leading references of African-American music in this country, with different bands running in parallel. A profound connoisseur and devotee of African-American roots music, he is also passionate about crime fiction and other cultural pursuits, which he has brought together with his music in various literary, theatrical and musical projects.
