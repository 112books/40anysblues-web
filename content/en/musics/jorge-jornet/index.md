He fell in love with the blues at first sight at the age of fourteen, when he saw a guitar-and-harmonica duo playing in a bar which the very next day became the [Carajillo Blues Trio](/en/bands/carajillo-blues-trio/), an outfit that from the outset opted for authenticity and the underground: “With a couple of carts, we went from bar to bar playing without a prior appointment.” There he learned his trade as a musician and began an artistic journey that has led him to become an unavoidable figure on the Barcelona blues scene and to play with local and international names such as [Joan Pau Cumellas](/en/musics/joan-pau-cumellas/), [José Luis Pardo](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Luis_Pardo), [Antonio Serrano](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Serrano_(m%C3%BAsico)), Alex Zayas, [Carl Weathersby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Weathersby), [Larry McCray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_McCray) or Slam Allen. His mastery and professionalism have led him to play with line-ups and projects beyond the genre, such as [La Cabra Mecánica](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Cabra_Mec%C3%A1nica), Chivo Chivato or Hot Swing Machine, but it was the blues that allowed him to express himself sentimentally through music: “Pentatonic scales are wonderful for expressing yourself simply, without complexes. It also gave me attitude.”
