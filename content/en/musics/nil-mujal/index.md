He first stepped onto a stage at sixteen, with a group playing their own songs and an orchestra from his home town. At eighteen he broadened his musical training in order to become a professional. He came to the blues through jazz and through playing with musicians who straddled both styles. A virtuoso and versatile player, he has played with more than fifty ensembles of different styles: jazz, blues, soul or reggae, always seduced by the undeniable connection between the blues and jazz: “The way they intertwine and allow you to play with the music, whether in a more visceral or a more reflective manner.” And this is borne out by his artistic references, which include [Lester Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_Young), [Sonny Rollins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonny_Rollins), [Duke Ellington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Ellington), [Billie Holliday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Holiday) or [John Coltrane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Coltrane). This command of African-American music has allowed him to accompany artists from the Barcelona scene such as Eduard Gener, [Chino Swingslide](/en/musics/hernan-serna/) or [Emma Fernández](/en/musics/emma-fernandez/). In his own words, the blues has given him expressiveness, sincerity, a direct connection with the audience and a personal and artistic world: “It has given me the chance to discover great musicians and colleagues, whom I would surely never have come across had it not been for this scene.”
