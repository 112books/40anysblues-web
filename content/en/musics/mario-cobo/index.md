Recognised as one of the finest guitarists and one of the most creative musicians in rock’n’roll and [rockabilly](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockabilly), he came to the blues by travelling back along his own sonic route, spurred on by the curiosity born of a [Mississippi John Hurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_John_Hurt) record that was knocking about his parents’ house. In love with the guitar from a very early age, he was always clear that he wanted to devote himself to music, and in 1996 he began his professional career with [The Nu Niles](/en/bands/the-nu-niles/), a legendary band on the blues and rock’n’roll scene in our country. His command of a variety of genres such as rhythm&blues, swing, jazz or country has allowed him to play with international artists such as [Dale Hawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Hawkins), [Janis Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janis_Martin), Kim Lenz and [Mike Sanchez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Sanchez). A deep connoisseur of American roots music, and displaying a kindly, ironic humour, he shows himself more seduced by the depth of the message and its universal language than by fireworks: “I am only interested in the blues when it is used to communicate, to entertain and to connect with the audience. When it focuses on virtuosity or on pure, hard stylistic exercises, I shut up shop and go back to my old vinyl records.”
