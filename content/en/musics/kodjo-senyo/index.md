A member of the Ewe people, with a long tradition of singing and oral transmission, he began playing self-taught as a teenager, curiously influenced by the black music of the other side of the Atlantic: blues, jazz, soul and funk. He began performing professionally in 1995, but it was after his arrival in Catalonia, in 2001, that he set out on a reverse journey and took on the influence of the traditionalist singers and of the black diaspora, fusing the language and music of his people with rhythms from artists such as [Tracy Chapman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Chapman), [Ry Cooder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ry_Cooder) and [Lokua Kanza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokua_Kanza), or, as he usually puts it: “My music begins when the diaspora meets the root”. Settled in Barcelona in 2014, he describes himself as an “Ewe singer-songwriter” and asserts the ancestral roots of his blues: “I didn't decide to play this genre. It is the music that comes out of me naturally. We all know that the blues in its current form was born in Africa. I can say that I was born in the cradle of the blues”. A genre that defines him intimately: “I think it has allowed me to be what I have always been”.
