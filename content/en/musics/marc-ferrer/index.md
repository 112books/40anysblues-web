Two compilation records by [Ray Charles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Charles) and [B.B. King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._B._King) that were lying around at home were to blame for an early taste for African-American roots music. He began playing the piano at five and by sixteen he was already studying jazz and playing in combos into which a blues number would now and then slip. He followed in the wake of pioneers such as Harmònica Zúmel, [Amadeu Casas](/en/musics/amadeu-casas/), [August Tharrats](/en/musics/august-tharrats/) and [Big Mama](/en/musics/big-mama-montse/), and in 1996 he joined [The Big Jamboree](/en/bands/the-big-jamboree/), where he remains to this day. His piano has accompanied artists such as [Big Dani Pérez](/en/musics/big-dani-perez/), [Agustí Burriel](/en/musics/agusti-burriel/), Laia Porta, Ricard Gili and [Víctor Puertas](/en/musics/victor-puertas/), among others, and international figures such as [Dale Hawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Hawkins) or [Barrence Whitfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrence_Whitfield). A regular at Spanish and European festivals, he has left a pedagogical mark through his classes at the Escola de Blues de Barcelona, passing on to his pupils, besides musical mastery, his love for a style he regards as much more than a musical genre: “It is history, tradition and feeling in their purest state. I am fascinated by everything that can be expressed by playing the blues.”
