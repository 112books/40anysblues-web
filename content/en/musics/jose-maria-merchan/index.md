His musical path began by listening to blues records and putting together the band METAFORA! with his friend [Amadeu Casas](/en/musics/amadeu-casas/), with whom he also shared the pioneering early years of [La Cova del Drac](/en/sales/la-cova-del-drac/). He began performing professionally in the late sixties at La Pèrgola de Montjuïc, a historic Barcelona venue, with black musicians of various origins: the United States, England, Africa or Cuba. In the early seventies he took part in line-ups of all kinds, such as [Los Amaya](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Amaya) or Esqueixada Sniff. A proof of his long career is his first number, “La Porta Blues”, written in the sixties and which he still plays with his current band, [Merchan & Co.](/en/bands/merchan--co./) The blues, as he himself explains, has given him magical moments such as the night in 1976 when he met [Muddy Waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muddy_Waters) after a concert at the Palau de la Musica, taking part in a jam at the Magic club with [Bob Margolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Margolin), but above all it has given him a sentimental grounding: “It has given me feeling, a way of life, friends and a great deal of music. When I play a solo, I play the blues. I don’t know how to do anything else.”
