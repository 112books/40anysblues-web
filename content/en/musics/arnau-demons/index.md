He is one of the latest additions to the Barcelona scene, coming from the Escola de Blues de Barcelona, where he enrolled before the minimum age thanks to his father's wishes: his father passed on to him, as he did to his sister, the singer [Carla Sunday](/en/musics/carla-sunday/), a passion for the genre. His first teacher and influence was the pianist [David Giorcelli](/en/musics/david-giorcelli-/), who, in his own words, helped him to achieve a very personal style. In 2021 he began to travel through the devil's music together with his sister and the drummer Héctor Infieles, with whom he set up the band [The Heeks](/en/bands/the-heeks/) to play covers of blues classics, a music whose vibrations and energy are what chiefly attract him and, by his own admission, “its musically crazy nights”. His references span a wide range of African-American music, from [Etta James](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etta_James) to [Otis Spann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otis_Spann), by way of [Ruth Brown](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_Brown), [Ray Charles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Charles) or [Freddie King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freddie_King), which have hooked him on a genre that has opened up for him a new horizon and a new personal environment: “It has allowed me to meet and learn from very talented and slightly surreal people”.
