That [Louis Armstrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Armstrong), [Etta James](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etta_James), [Big Joe Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Joe_Turner) and [Blue Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Mitchell) number among his main favourite artists gives a clear idea of the depth and breadth of the knowledge this talented instrumentalist has of the genre, which he began to discover just as he began to take a real interest in music. After decades of being an essential part of the Barcelona scene, he describes his professional evolution with overwhelming simplicity and naturalness:

 “You start out as an amateur, you like it, you begin to get paid work and suddenly you decide that this is the life you are looking for”. A virtuoso, elegant and eclectic, his broad range has led him to collaborate with artists and bands of styles as diverse as the Salseta del [Poble Sec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Poble-sec,_Barcelona), Traffic, Myriam Swanson or Lucrecia, always carrying within him the spirit of the blues: “Which manages to draw beauty out of painful things” and which has offered him a way of communicating: “To be able to express myself and feel, and to feel how others express themselves and feel. To communicate, in the end”.
