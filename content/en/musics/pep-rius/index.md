His approach to the blues runs parallel to his first musical steps: “It was almost the first thing I learned to play on the guitar. Then I started on the double bass, I went deeper into jazz, and since then I have been combining the two styles and the two instruments.” This versatility has made him an essential part of the African-American music scene ever since, in 1994, he joined [La Locomotora Negra](/en/bands/la-locomotora-negra/), the historic Catalan jazz band, where he became a professional musician. In love with “the sound, the language, the *feeling* and even the iconography” of roots black music, this virtuoso of the strings has also developed an important educational side as a music teacher and illustrator of textbooks, over the course of a career in which he has worked with the most relevant national and international artists. Inspired by [Ray Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Brown_(musician)), [Big Bill Broonzy](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Bill_Broonzy), [Brownie McGhee](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brownie_McGhee) and [Josh White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_White), after a long professional journey, African-American roots music has left him with a personal mark: “The chance to meet very special people and to be able to play with them. Connecting through music.”
