The blues crossed his path in the shape of his brother [Cesc Miró](/en/musics/cesc-miro/), the trumpeter of [The Big Jamboree](/en/bands/the-big-jamboree/), who invited him to join the band after he had bought his first baritone sax. As he himself explains, it was not a decision, it was an opportunity. In the first two decades of this century he has accompanied the most outstanding artists of the Barcelona scene with his baritone, such as [Wax & Boogie](/en/bands/wax--boogie/), [Emma Fernández](/en/musics/emma-fernandez/) or [Chino Swingslide](/en/musics/hernan-serna/) & [The Big Bet](/en/bands/chino-swingslide--the-big-bet/). In love with the classic rhythm & blues of the forties and fifties: “The blackest stuff, with a good horn section, the rhythm section, a piano and a slightly broken guitar, drives me crazy.” With his references firmly anchored in the classics such as [Louis Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Jordan), [Leo Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Parker) or [Big Jay McNeely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Jay_McNeely), he states that the best gift music has given him has been enjoying the friendship of the professionals he has met along the way, also collaborating with them as an arranger: “I am a people person. I like projects not so much for the songs as for what my companions are like and how they dazzle me with their art.”
