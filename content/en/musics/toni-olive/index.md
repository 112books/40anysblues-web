He belongs to the group of pioneers of Barcelona blues who grew up with [Cream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cream_(band)), [John Mayall](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Mayall) or [Jimi Hendrix](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimi_Hendrix). In his city he began by listening to outfits such as [Màquina!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A0quina!), Vértice or [Toti Soler](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toti_Soler): “I did it without knowing that it was the blues. And so did [Quico Pi de la Serra](/en/musics/quico-pi-de-la-serra/), who in his performances paid tribute to [Big Bill Broonzy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Bill_Broonzy).” He began performing in the early seventies, but it was his encounter with [Vicente Zúmel](/en/musics/vicente-zumel/) that led him to African-American roots music. “He invited me into his blues bands, and in the mid-eighties we formed the seminal [Harmònica Zúmel Blues Band](/en/bands/harm%C3%B2nica-zumel-blues-band/), made up of Zúmel himself, [Carles Slide Nadal](/en/musics/carles-nadal/), Julian Garcia, Pere Sabaté and myself.” These are the first steps of the county’s blues, and he recalls them with nostalgic precision: “We played great gigs and jams, joined by the likes of [August Tharrats](/en/musics/august-tharrats/), [Big Mama Montse](/en/musics/big-mama-montse/), [Ricky Gil](/en/musics/ricky-gil/), [Amadeu Casas](/en/musics/amadeu-casas/) or Carlos Segarra, for example, and we had the honour of sharing a stage with figures such as [Memphis Slim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis_Slim), [B.B. King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.B._King), [Champion Jack Dupree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champion_Jack_Dupree) or [Maurice John Vaughn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_John_Vaughn). And ever since, he has remained in love with the apparent yet profound simplicity of a genre whose capacity for connection he values above all: “Being able to play with people and communicate with them instantly, almost without knowing each other, and to see that the music flows and that you understand each other perfectly.”
