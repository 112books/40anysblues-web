His restlessness in exploring new and diverse cultural and musical territories eventually led him to the roots of African-American music: “That is how I found the blues, which, coming from jazz, was waiting for me on a street corner to make friends”, he explains himself. He has played the bass since the age of twenty, and it was the historic guitarist [Amadeu Casas](/en/musics/amadeu-casas/) who gave him his first opportunities as a professional musician, almost a couple of decades ago. A great connoisseur of the genre and a lover of a wide-spectrum blues, from [Robert Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Johnson_(musician)) and [Freddie King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freddie_King) to [Keb Mo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keb%27_Mo%27) or [John Mayer](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Mayer), over the last few decades he has accompanied the very best of the genre in Barcelona, the city where he has made a place for himself and surrounded himself with the best thing, by his own admission, that music has given him: his friends. On the style he one day decided to play, he confesses that he likes absolutely all of it, especially its capacity to transmit energy, feelings and ideas: “The predictable and at the same time hypnotic rhythm and harmony, which catch you in a mantra, and on the other hand, the melodic and poetic language that extends, practically, through all of today's popular music”.
