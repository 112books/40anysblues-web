It happened on a night in 2011 in one of the city’s most emblematic clubs, the [Rocksound](/en/sales/rock-sound/). A young singer was presenting herself at the SBB competition with a band put together for the occasion with the best of each house. Ster did not win, but she did make the definitive leap into professionalism. With a striking voice, an image that transports you back to the glory days of rhythm & blues and a dazzling ease on stage, in a decade she went from a chorus in burlesque shows to top-billed performer, becoming one of the finest female voices in the country. Precociously famed on stage, she began singing with a female [doo-wop](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doo-wop) group, then formed [Ster and The Waxers](/en/bands/ster-and-the-waxers/), took part in Burlesque Taboo and finally consolidated [Wax & Boogie](/en/bands/wax--boogie/) alongside the pianist [David Giorcelli](/en/musics/david-giorcelli-/). Her secret weapon, by her own admission, is “the ability to turn the stage into my own home, or at least to convey that”. Sincere, demanding and hard-working, she manages to win the audience over by putting her whole soul into the music she performs and by seeking out the roots in a range of classics that runs from [Ruth Brown](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_Brown) to [Mose Allison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mose_Allison), by way of [Etta James](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etta_James), [Alicia Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicia_Keys), [Stevie Wonder](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevie_Wonder) and [Ray Charles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Charles), among the hundreds of musicians who shape the artistic universe of this singer.
