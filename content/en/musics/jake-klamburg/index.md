He arrived in Barcelona at the age of five, studied at the Liceu conservatoire and at the Taller de Músics, and began working as a session musician at nineteen, after cutting his teeth playing in rock bands of various kinds. But his landing in the world of the blues came about when he shared a flat with Mattia Sinigaglia and Pol Jubany, founders of the group [La Testapazza](/en/bands/la-testapazza/), winners of one of the first competitions organised by the SBB and where his career in the genre began. In little more than a decade he has become a familiar figure on the Barcelona scene, playing with different bands and collaborating with leading national and international musicians such as Thomas Toussaint, Mike Keller or [Keith Dunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Dunn_%28musician%29). He states that in the blues he has found a certain roughness and “truth” about different aspects of life, and that it has allowed him to grow as a musician and as a person: “Above all, what it has given me on a personal level are the experiences I have lived through with all my colleagues, which have made me the person I am now”.
