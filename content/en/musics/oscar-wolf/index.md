Of all the new Barcelona blues generation, he is undoubtedly the one who practises the most genuine side of it, the style closest to what used to be heard in the _juke joints_ of the Mississippi, as can easily be deduced from his confessed influences, from [Blind Lemon Jefferson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blind_Lemon_Jefferson) to [Big Bill Broonzy](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Bill_Broonzy), by way of [Lonnie Johnson](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lonnie_Johnson), [Furry Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furry_Lewis), [Blind Willie McTell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blind_Willie_McTell), [Tampa Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa_Red), [Eddie Lang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddie_Lang), [Django Reinhardt](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Django_Reinhardt), [Bessie Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessie_Smith) and a long etcetera of pre-war musicians. An introspective artist, his way of playing and singing carries a strong charge of authenticity that connects directly with his way of approaching the genre: “It was a style I connected with at a particular moment in my life, when I was going through a considerable existential crisis, and I realised that this was the music that was in tune with what I was feeling”. He began playing in 2006 with the group [Flash Cocodrilos](/en/bands/flash-cocodrilos/), and since then, although he does not appear too often, he has become one of the most talented young artists on the blues scene, a music that is something more: “A way of understanding this world, so often so raw and hostile”.
