Matías Cipiliano was to blame, the guitar teacher who recommended that a young twenty-three-year-old Burguez try his luck with a singer who needed a blues guitarist. He was caught for ever and the following year he was already forming his first band, [El Club del Jump](/en/bands/el-club-del-jump/). In 2021 he settled in Barcelona and embarked on a meteoric career, collaborating with local musicians and creating two bands, [Martín Burguez & His Rhythm Combo](/en/bands/mart%C3%ADn-burguez--his-rhythm-combo/) and [The Groovy Four](/en/bands/the-groovy-four/), in which he displays an impeccable guitar touch, an excellent voice and an elegant stage presence. With a considerable knowledge of the genre, in his musical Olympus the brightest stars of electric blues reign supreme, from [T-Bone Walker](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-Bone_Walker) to [Jimmy Rogers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Rogers), passing through the three Kings (B.B., Freddie and Albert), [Jimmy Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Reed), [Johnny Guitar Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_%22Guitar%22_Watson) or [Guitar Slim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitar_Slim), and this shows in his ability to perform the most diverse styles, always offering a personal contribution: “It’s the path I chose years ago and the path I intend to follow, knowing the genre, learning from it and trying to seek a way of my own... as the artists I admire did.”
