This virtuoso guitarist, like so many others, travelled the reverse path of black music and, setting out from [rockabilly](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockabilly), ended up arriving at the blues. He settled in Barcelona in the late nineties and here he studied the guitar and became a figure of [jazz manouche](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz_manouche), with some highly praised compositions of his own and original, uniting tradition with a personal and innovative vision. Passion, joined to technique, has allowed him to carve out an international reputation for himself, on a par with [Stochelo Rosenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochelo_Rosenberg) or [Christian Escoudé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Escoud%C3%A9), performing at the most prestigious festivals. He has also taken part in compilation recordings of the best [gypsy jazz](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gypsy_jazz) in the world and his music has reached the cinema with the soundtrack of [Woody Allen](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Allen)’s film _Vicky, Cristina, Barcelona_. A devotee of black roots music, he moves with absolute assurance through the music of New Orleans, [traditional jazz](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz_tradicional), Balkan music or the blues, a music he admires for its depth and versatility: “Honestly: the blues scale seems to me a stunning combination of sounds. [Henri Mancini](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Mancini) can use it and create atomic melodies; [John Lee Hooker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lee_Hooker) can use it and give meaning to his human existence”.
