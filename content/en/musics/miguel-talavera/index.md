He had his first guitar at the age of six, and although his first approach to it was through flamenco, he discovered African-American roots music listening to rock’n’roll records but above all through the film _Crossroads_, by [Walter Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Hill), which he saw when he was only ten. Since then he remained captivated by the devil's music and gave his first concert at sixteen: “It was at a Sant Joan's Eve party, it wasn't a great concert, but it was a good start”. His mastery of both the acoustic and the electric guitar, an instrument he learned in a self-taught way, has made him an outstanding figure on the Barcelona blues scene, in which this specialist of the fingerpicking technique, with a flamenco-tinged touch, moves like a fish in water, with the respect of his fellow professionals. A versatile instrumentalist, he moves along the most guitar-driven path of the blues, a style that gives him the possibility of expressing his feelings and of which he confesses that what catches him is its more passionate side: “The feeling, the sincerity, the emotion, the melancholy and above all the passion that this music conveys”.
